Real data decides what gets built, not guesswork

Every market page on this site starts from a real, ranked dataset — 2025 HMDA loan-level origination data and U.S. Census ACS estimates across all 1,798 U.S. counties. The system tells me which markets actually have the HELOC volume and free-and-clear equity to justify a dedicated page, and I decide which ones we build.

Shipped, not just planned

Every new market page reuses the same compliance-reviewed intake form, byte for byte — only the local numbers and copy change, and I write those myself rather than mail-merging the same four sentences across every county. Nothing goes live without me reviewing the real figures first.

Why it matters for you

Most lender sites run one generic page for the whole country. Equoro's pages are built market by market, with the real numbers for where you actually live — because the system that builds them was designed by someone who's spent a career doing this work, not a marketing template.
