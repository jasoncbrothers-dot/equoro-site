Privacy Notice

How Equoro handles your information.

Last updated September 2026

Who this notice covers

This notice applies to equoro.ai and to the intake form linked from it. Equoro is operated by Jason Brothers, NMLS # 829954, under SunnyHill Financial Inc., a licensed mortgage broker, Company NMLS # 1708856.

What we collect

  • What you enter in the intake form: property details, estimated value and mortgage balance, how the property is owned and used, the purpose of the funds, and a self-reported credit score range.
  • Contact details you provide: name, phone number, and email address.
  • The timestamped record of the consent language you saw and agreed to when you submitted the form.

How we use it

  • To evaluate which of our lending partners’ programs may fit your situation.
  • To share your information with lending partners whose programs fit — only after you’ve given consent, never before.
  • To contact you about your submission, at your request.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your information.
  • We don’t share it with a lending partner before you’ve consented.
  • We don’t use auto-dialers or telemarketers to reach you.

Your choices

You can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to any message from us, or by calling or emailing us directly using the details on our Contact page. You can also ask what information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it, subject to any record-keeping requirements that apply to licensed mortgage brokers.

Security

Information you submit through the intake form travels over an encrypted connection. Access to your information is limited to what’s needed to evaluate and process your submission.

Questions

Reach Jason Brothers directly through the Contact page.
