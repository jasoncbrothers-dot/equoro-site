Equity, plainly

What you own, minus what you owe.

Home equity is the part of your home’s value that isn’t covered by a mortgage. A home equity line of credit or loan lets you borrow against a portion of it, using the home as collateral.

Lenders don’t lend against all of it. Each sets its own ceiling on how much of the home’s value can be borrowed in total, and that ceiling — along with your credit, income and the property itself — is what decides the number you’re offered. The example here is illustrative, not a quote.

What is a HELOC?

A home equity line of credit is a revolving line, secured by your home, that you can draw from as needed up to a set limit — a bit like a credit card backed by your house. Most have a draw period when you can borrow and often pay interest only, followed by a repayment period when you pay down what you used. Rates are commonly variable, so payments can change.

How is a HELOC different from a home equity loan?

A home equity loan is a lump sum, usually at a fixed rate with a fixed monthly payment, paid back over a set term. A HELOC is a line you draw on over time, usually at a variable rate. One-time costs with a known amount often suit a loan; ongoing or uncertain costs often suit a line. Neither is universally better.

Will checking my options affect my credit?

Telling Equoro about your home and plans is the first step, not a loan application — we ask for a credit score range, not permission to pull your report. When you choose to work with a lending partner, that partner will tell you what they check and when, before it happens. Any step that could affect your credit is theirs to explain, and yours to say yes to.

Is Equoro a lender?

Equoro is a licensed mortgage broker. We shop your file across the lenders on our panel and bring back the options that fit — final rates, terms and approval decisions are the lender’s to make, and they’re required to disclose them to you before you commit. Nothing on this page is an offer or commitment to lend.

Does it cost anything to use Equoro?

There’s no charge to check your options with Equoro. If you go on to take a loan or line of credit, the lender’s own costs and terms apply, and they’re required to disclose them to you before you commit. Equoro may be compensated by its lending partners; that never changes which partners you see or the order you see them in.

What if my home is owned by a trust or an LLC?

You can still check your options. Tell us in the ownership step so we match you with partners who work with that structure. Later, a partner will ask for a trust certification (for a trust) or the operating agreement and EIN (for an LLC). You don’t need any of that today.
